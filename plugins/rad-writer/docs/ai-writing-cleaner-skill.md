# Coding Agent Skill: Humanized Writing & AI Tell Elimination

## Core Purpose
This skill configures an AI agent or chat assistant to write and format documents that are indistinguishable from high-quality, professional human writing. It acts as a strict filter to eliminate predictable statistical patterns, overused vocabulary, rhythmic monotony, and technical formatting tells typical of Large Language Models (LLMs).

---

## 1. The 2026 Word & Phrase Blacklist

To avoid triggering AI content detectors and human "AI fatigue," you must run a strict search-and-replace audit on your drafts. **Never use the words on the left; replace them with the active, concrete human alternatives on the right.**

### A. General Categorized Blacklist
| Category | Banned AI Words / Phrases | Approved Human Alternatives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intros & Openers** | *Delve, Dive into, In today's digital age, At its core, Imagine a world, Demystify, Navigate* | Look at, Explore, Now, Basically, Suppose, [Start with the core conflict] |
| **Corporate Buzzwords**| *Leverage, Synergy, Paradigm shift, Robust, Scalable, Cutting-edge, Elevate, Navigate, Optimize, Streamline* | Use, Working together, Big change, Strong, Reliable, New, Cut costs, Work faster |
| **Transitions** | *Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, In conclusion, At the end of the day, Ultimately, In essence, It is important to note* | Also, Plus, Besides, To wrap up, Keep in mind, [Use logical flow of ideas] |
| **High Drama** | *Transformative, Revolutionary, Game-changer, Unleash, Unlock, Testament* | Impactful, Major, Helpful, Start using, Access, Proof |
| **Vague Adjectives** | *Myriad, Plethora, Comprehensive, Pivotal, Unwavering, Multifaceted, Crucial, Enduring, Valuable, Vibrant* | Many, Lots of, Full, Key, Steady, Complex, Essential, Lasting, Useful |
| **Flowery Metaphors** | *Tapestry, Landscape, Beacon, Journey, Roadmap, Symphony, Nestled, In the heart of* | Mix, Situation, Guide, Process, Plan, Blend, Located in |

### B. Verb & Adjective Overuse Audit
*   **Banned Verbs:** *Foster, Ignite, Empower, Uncover, Unleash, Underscore, Optimize, Streamline.*
    *   *AI Style:* "We empower users to optimize their workflows."
    *   *Human Style:* "We help you work faster" or "We cut your server costs by 30%."
*   **Banned Adjectives:** *Seamless, Robust, Future-ready, Multifaceted, Pivotal, Dynamic.*
    *   *Note on "Seamless":* This is a placeholder for a missing feature description. Replace it with the exact details of how the integration or feature works.

### C. Metaphorical & Spatial Tells
*   AI models are statistically obsessed with spatial and fabric metaphors.
    *   **Banned Metaphors:** *weaving a tapestry, navigating complex ecosystems, a beacon of hope in the landscape of [X].*
    *   *Human alternative:* State the relationship directly (e.g., "integrate your tech stack" instead of "weaving a tapestry of solutions").

### D. Reclaiming the Copula ("Is" / "Are")
*   AI overcomplicates basic sentences to sound sophisticated, avoiding simple forms of "to be" or "to have." **Forced Override:**
    *   **Banned Indirections:** *serves as, stands as, marks, functions as, operates as, represents [a], boasts, features, maintains, offers.*
    *   *AI Style:* "The dashboard serves as a central hub."
    *   *Human Style:* "The dashboard is a central hub."
    *   *AI Style:* "The software boasts a modern interface."
    *   *Human Style:* "The software has a modern interface."

### E. Vague Connections
*   AI abstracts connections rather than explaining them.
    *   **Banned Abstracts:** *in connection with, connected to, in association with, associated with.*
    *   *Human alternative:* Define the relationship precisely (*"working with," "used for," "caused by"*).

## 2. Rhythm, Syntax, and Burstiness Rules

AI models tend to play it safe, regressing to a statistical mean that produces uniform, low-burstiness sentence structures. You must intentionally inject "jaggedness" into your writing.

### A. Break "The Balanced Paragraph"
*   **The Tell:** AI produces perfectly rectangular paragraphs consisting of 3–4 sentences, each exactly 15–20 words long, in a rigid Subject-Verb-Object pattern.
*   **The Fix (High-Low Technique):** Intentionally vary sentence lengths. Follow a long, complex, explanatory sentence with a short, sharp punch. Like this.
*   **Grammatical Risks:** Humans write with fragments, or start sentences with *But* or *And*. Do not sanitise the writing to the point of clinical perfection.

### B. Eliminate "Negative Parallelisms"
*   AI constantly uses contrasting structures to clear up imaginary misconceptions.
    *   **Banned Patterns:** *Not only X, but also Y*; *It is not just X, it's Y*; *No X. No Y. Just Z.* (e.g., "No hardware. No fees. Just growth.").
    *   *Instruction:* If a contrast is necessary, use a direct, active statement.

### C. Break "Listicle Formatting"
*   **The Tell:** AI almost always formats vertical lists with a `**Bold Header**: followed by an explanatory sentence.` It rarely mixes list styles.
*   **The Fix:**
    1.  Turn lists into a continuous narrative paragraph.
    2.  Use nested bullets.
    3.  Use numbered lists that do *not* follow a colon-heavy "Header: Description" template.

### D. Avoid the "Rule of Three"
*   AI overuses triplets to make lists or descriptions feel comprehensive (e.g., *"tiles, metals, and plastics"* or *"reducing footprint, improving efficiency, and fostering development"*). Limit the use of three consecutive adjectives or phrases.

## 3. Stance, Authority, and Content Directives

LLMs are trained via RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) to be harmless and neutral, which translates into weak, non-committal writing. B2B thought leadership and strong copywriting require a definitive viewpoint.

### A. The First 10% Deletion Rule (Throat-Clearing)
*   AI loves polite, academic introductions (e.g., *"This article aims to explore..."*, *"In this document, we will discuss..."*).
*   **The Instruction:** Delete the entire first paragraph or introductory sentence. Start directly in the second paragraph with the central conflict, high-stakes fact, or narrative hook.
    *   *AI:* "In this article, we will discuss the importance of churn reduction."
    *   *Human:* "If you don't fix your churn by Q3, your acquisition budget is at risk."

### B. Ban Safety Hedges
*   Eliminate phrases that signal authority-killing hesitation.
    *   **Banned Hedges:** *It is important to consider, While it is true, It could be argued that, Generally speaking, As of [date], Up to my last training update.*

### C. Avoid the "Both-Sides Fallacy"
*   Do not list generic pros and cons to stay neutral. Commit to a clear, defensible stance on the subject matter.

### D. Avoid "Fake E-E-A-T / Hypothesizing"
*   AI attempts to fake experience using generic scenarios.
    *   **Banned Openers:** *Picture this...*, *Imagine a scenario...*, *As a business owner, you know...*
    *   *The Instruction:* Use actual, gritty, specific real-world details instead.
        *   *Generic AI:* "Imagine a busy kitchen where things go wrong."
        *   *Gritty Human:* "When the POS goes down on a Friday at 7 PM and the kitchen is understaffed..."

### E. Stop "Puffing Up" Significance
*   AI desperately attempts to link minor details to global movements.
    *   **Banned Phrases:** *marking a pivotal moment, represented a significant shift, part of a broader movement, solidifying its role as a hub, raising philosophical questions, serves as a testament.*
    *   *The Instruction:* State facts exactly as they are without adding unearned historical or ecological significance.

## 4. Technical Formatting & Platform Leakage Defense

These are dead giveaways of a copy-paste job from a chatbot. **You must actively scan for and remove the following artifacts:**

### A. Internal LLM Reference Bugs
*   **ChatGPT Code:** Delete any instances of:
    *   `contentReference[oaicite:X]{index=Y}` or `[oai_citation:X‡source]`
    *   `turn0searchX`, `turn0imageX`, or `turn1fileX` surrounded by Unicode
    *   JSON formatting code at the ends of sentences, e.g., `({"attribution":{"attributableIndex":"X-Y"}})`
    *   URLs appended with UTM parameters like `?utm_source=chatgpt.com` or `?utm_source=openai`
*   **Gemini Code:** Delete any instances of `[cite: X]` or formatting bugs like `[span_X](start_span)` and `[span_X](end_span)`.
*   **Grok Code:** Delete tags like `<grok-card>` or JSON attributes like `grok_render_citation_card_json=...` and URLs with `referrer=grok.com`.
*   **DeepSeek Code:** Delete lenticular brackets and daggers, such as `【85†L261-269】`.
*   **Perplexity Code:** Delete tags like `[attached_file:X]` or `[web:X]`, and URLs containing `ppl-ai-file-upload`.
*   **Unclassified Code:** Delete triple-colon document wrappers, e.g., `:::writing{variant="document" id="XXXXX"}`.

### B. Typography & Markdown Overuse
*   **No Title Headings:** Do not repeat the document's main title inside the text as an H1 heading.
*   **No Title Case Headings:** Keep your section headings in sentence case (only capitalize the first word and proper nouns). AI strongly defaults to Title Case.
*   **Heading Structure:** Never skip heading levels (e.g., going from H1 directly to H3). Never create H1 headings inside the body, and never nest headings without body text between them.
*   **Standardize Quotations:** AI defaults to curly quotation marks (`“...”`) and curly apostrophes (`’`). Revert these to straight quotes (`"..."`) and straight apostrophes (`'`) to match developers' or clean plain-text formatting.
*   **Eliminate Emojis as Bullets:** Do not decorate section headers or list bullet points with emojis.
*   **Limit Em Dashes:** Avoid the mechanical overuse of em dashes (`—`). If used, never surround them with spaces.
*   **No Excessive Boldface:** Do not mechanically apply bold formatting to random key phrases in a paragraph (e.g. `**private equity firms**` and `**financial sponsors**`). Bold should be used sparingly for visual rhythm, not as a reading highlighter.

## 5. Execution Protocol: The Human Sandwich Workflow

Ensure this three-step workflow is enforced on every single generation task:

1.  **Human Input (Strategy & Data):** Provide the core data points, proprietary studies, or real-life anecdotes.
2.  **AI Execution (Drafting):** Use this skill to write a factual, clean, low-probability draft.
3.  **Human Polish (The Pub Test):** Read the final sentence aloud. *Would a person say this to a colleague over a beer?* If a sentence sounds over-sophisticated, awkward, or too rigid, revert it to a simple, direct statement.
