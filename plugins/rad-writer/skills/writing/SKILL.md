# Writing

Improve text for its actual reader, purpose, channel, and voice. Aim for clear, specific, accurate prose. This skill supports good writing. It does not hide AI use or claim human authorship.

## Apply

1. Identify the document type, audience, purpose, channel, voice, and hard limits. Ask only when a missing fact could change the result.
2. Read [writing-standards.md](references/writing-standards.md) and use the section that matches the work. If several modes apply, choose the primary one and keep the shared checks.
3. Preserve the user's meaning, facts, numbers, citations, uncertainty, plot facts, and requested format. Never invent evidence, sources, personal experience, or story events. Mark missing support clearly.
4. Edit for direct meaning, concrete detail, natural rhythm, and reader fit. Keep a valid voice when a source uses deliberate unusual wording.
5. Return the requested text first. Add an editorial note only when a missing fact, citation, or choice materially affects the result.

## Shared review

- Cut throat-clearing, empty praise, vague attributions, inflated claims, stock metaphors, and generic assistant remarks.
- Prefer exact nouns and active verbs. Use simple forms such as `is`, `has`, and `shows` when they are correct.
- Vary sentence length and paragraph shape naturally. Do not force short-sentence quotas, fragments, flashbacks, or word substitutions that harm meaning.
- Keep formatting useful. Use sentence-case headings, restrained emphasis, simple lists, and no decorative emojis unless requested.
- Remove accidental platform markers such as `contentReference`, `oai_citation`, `[cite: ...]`, or `turn0search...`. Keep legitimate citations, links, code, and quoted text.
